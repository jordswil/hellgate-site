Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Bundle ID: dev.jordswil.hellgate

The short version

HELLGATE collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting service or third-party data collection. Gameplay and save data stay on your device.

What the app stores

  • Save data—campaign progress, Forge and Armory state, and settings such as text size and reduced motion—is stored only on your device in the app's local storage.
  • Your save never leaves your device unless you choose Settings → Save data → Export. That copies a text code to your clipboard for you to keep or share yourself.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. Apple's iCloud device backup may include it as part of your normal device backup; Apple's privacy policy governs that service.

What the app does not do

  • No gameplay, analytics, advertising or account network requests. The game is fully playable in Airplane Mode.
  • No collection of personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, advertising, account or data-collection SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases and no messaging between players.

If you tap Privacy Policy, Support, Rate HELLGATE or an Apple refund link, iOS opens that public page outside the game.

Purchases

HELLGATE is sold as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. Apple is the merchant of record: Apple processes payment, holds your payment details and handles refunds. We receive sales reports from Apple that contain no information identifying individual customers. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is rated 13+ for fantasy violence. Because it collects no data, no personal information is collected from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe this policy is wrong or have a question, email jordswilson@hotmail.com.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that collects data—for example, optional Game Center leaderboards—this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated in the same release, and the change will be described in that version's release notes.
