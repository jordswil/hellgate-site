HELLGATE Support

HELLGATE is a premium, fully offline dark-fantasy tower defence game for iPhone and iPad.

Frequently asked questions

My save disappeared or I got a new device. How do I restore progress?

Progress is stored only on your device; there is no account or cloud sync. On the old device, open Settings → Save data → Export. Keep or send the copied text code to yourself. On the new device, open Settings → Save data, paste the code and tap Import. Keep an export before deleting the app. A full iCloud or Finder device backup may also restore the save.

The import says the code is invalid.

Make sure you copied the entire code—it is one long line without spaces. Codes from a newer game version may not import into an older version, so update both devices first. If it still fails, email the first and last 10 characters of the code and your build number; do not email the full save code unless asked.

Does HELLGATE work offline?

Yes. The complete game is playable in Airplane Mode.

Are there in-app purchases, ads or tracking?

No. One App Store purchase includes the full game. There are no ads, in-app purchases or tracking. The Fortune Deck, relics and General Store items are earned using gameplay rewards.

Can I use Family Sharing?

Apple supports Family Sharing for eligible paid apps. Availability depends on the purchaser's Apple Family settings and storefront.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes all purchases and refunds. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, choose “I'd like to,” select “Request a refund,” pick a reason and choose HELLGATE. Australian and New Zealand consumer-law rights continue to apply.

The game drops frames or closes during a busy map.

HELLGATE targets iPhone 12 or newer and recent iPads. Try Settings → Reduced motion, close other apps and restart the device. If the problem continues, email your device model, iOS version, build number and map name.

Why won't the game rotate to portrait?

HELLGATE is landscape-only by design. Rotate your device to either landscape direction. On iPad, widen a small Split View or resizable window if the rotate/widen message appears.

What accessibility settings are available?

Settings includes Reduced Motion, colour-blind accents for lane and team colours, and four text sizes. Email suggestions for additional accessibility support.
